# Data

The original project used Landsat 8 OLI imagery and a study-area boundary.

The satellite raster data are **not stored in this repository**. The documentation records the acquisition dates, bands, CRS, and processing workflow.

### Landsat acquisitions

- Pre-fire: 28 July 2020
- Post-fire: 15 July 2021
- NIR: Band 5
- SWIR2: Band 7
- CRS: EPSG:32611

The report also describes an area of interest of approximately 30 km × 30 km.
